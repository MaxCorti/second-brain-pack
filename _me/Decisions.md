# Decisions journal

> One row per real decision the owner made (review feedback, conversation, daily note), so agents learn **why** they decide, not just what.
> - **Their words** are quoted exactly, never paraphrased. If there's no direct quote, write `(no quote)`.
> - **Why (inferred)** is the agent's reading and stays `inferred` until the owner confirms it. Then it becomes `stated` with the confirmation's date.
> - Distilled patterns go to [[Motivations]]. Newest rows last. `private:` content never goes here.
