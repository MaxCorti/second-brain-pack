# /interview-me

1. Read `_me/` (all of it; it's small) and `_me/Open Questions.md`.
2. Pick about 6–8 questions: unanswered Open Questions top-down, plus any `inferred` facts in `_me` worth confirming.
3. Ask 1–2 at a time, conversationally. Accept "skip". Ask follow-ups when an answer opens something important (goals, constraints, people).
4. After each answer, write it into the right `_me` note as `(stated, interview YYYY-MM-DD)`. Fix or mark `(superseded)` any `inferred` item it contradicts. Tick or remove the question, and add new questions that came up.
5. If an answer reveals an idea or an intention to act, handle it like /triage (idea note or Notion Inbox task, per `_system/notion-bridge.md`).
6. Update `updated:` dates, commit `interview YYYY-MM-DD`, and summarise what agents now know that they didn't before.
